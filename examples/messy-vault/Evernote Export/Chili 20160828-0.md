**Serves:** 6
**Time:** 17 minutes

**Ingredients**

- a pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 onions
- 400 g tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic
- 100 g butter
**Method**

1. Season and taste.
2. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
3. Bake until golden.
4. Whisk until smooth.
