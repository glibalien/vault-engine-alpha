**Serves:** 2
**Time:** 47 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 250 ml stock
- 2 eggs
- 200 g flour
- 400 g tomatoes
**Method**

1. Whisk until smooth.
2. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
3. Bake until golden.
4. Chop everything finely.
