**Serves:** 6
**Time:** 53 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 1 lemon
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- fresh parsley
- a pinch of salt
- 2 onions
**Method**

1. Chop everything finely.
2. Season and taste.
3. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
4. Bake until golden.
