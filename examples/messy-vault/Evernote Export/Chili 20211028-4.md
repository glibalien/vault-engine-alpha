**Serves:** 3
**Time:** 39 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- fresh parsley
- 1 tsp cumin
- 200 g flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 lemon
**Method**

1. Bake until golden.
2. Chop everything finely.
3. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
4. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
