**Serves:** 5
**Time:** 50 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 2 eggs
- 400 g tomatoes
- a pinch of salt
- 2 onions
- 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
**Method**

1. Bake until golden.
2. Chop everything finely.
3. Season and taste.
4. Soften the onions over a low heat.
