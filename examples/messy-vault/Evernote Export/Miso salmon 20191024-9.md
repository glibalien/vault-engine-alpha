**Serves:** 4
**Time:** 56 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 3 cloves garlic
- 100 g butter
- 1 lemon
- a pinch of salt
- 2 onions
- fresh parsley
**Method**

1. Whisk until smooth.
2. Season and taste.
3. Chop everything finely.
4. Soften the onions over a low heat.
