**Serves:** 4
**Time:** 32 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 2 onions
- 200 g flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- a pinch of salt
**Method**

1. Bake until golden.
2. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
3. Whisk until smooth.
4. Season and taste.
