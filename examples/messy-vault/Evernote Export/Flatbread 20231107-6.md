**Serves:** 5
**Time:** 23 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 3 cloves garlic
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon
- 250 ml stock
- a pinch of salt
**Method**

1. Chop everything finely.
2. Season and taste.
3. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
4. Whisk until smooth.
