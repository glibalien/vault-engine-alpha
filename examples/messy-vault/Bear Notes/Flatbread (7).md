# Flatbread
#recipe #imported

- Serves: 6
- Time: 46 minutes

### Ingredients
- 400 g tomatoes
- a pinch of salt
- fresh parsley
- 2 eggs
- 100 g butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
### Method
1. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
2. Chop everything finely.
3. Bake until golden.
4. Season and taste.
