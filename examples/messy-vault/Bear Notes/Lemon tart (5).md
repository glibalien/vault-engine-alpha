# Lemon tart
#recipe #imported

- Serves: 5
- Time: 40 minutes

### Ingredients
- 2 onions
- 1 tsp cumin
- 3 cloves garlic
- 100 g butter
- a pinch of salt
- 2 eggs
### Method
1. Bake until golden.
2. Chop everything finely.
3. Soften the onions over a low heat.
4. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
