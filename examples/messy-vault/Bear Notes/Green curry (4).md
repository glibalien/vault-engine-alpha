# Green curry
#recipe #imported

- Serves: 4
- Time: 70 minutes

### Ingredients
- 3 cloves garlic
- 250 ml stock
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 200 g flour
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 lemon
### Method
1. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
2. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
3. Soften the onions over a low heat.
4. Whisk until smooth.
