# Mushroom risotto
#recipe #imported

- Serves: 2
- Time: 60 minutes

### Ingredients
- 3 cloves garlic
- fresh parsley
- 2 eggs
- 100 g butter
- 250 ml stock
- 200 g flour
### Method
1. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
2. Add the liquid and simmer for twenty minutes.
3. Whisk until smooth.
4. Soften the onions over a low heat.
