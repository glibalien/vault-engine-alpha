# Roast chicken
#recipe #imported

- Serves: 5
- Time: 69 minutes

### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 250 ml stock
- 3 cloves garlic
- fresh parsley
- a pinch of salt
- 100 g butter
### Method
1. Rest for ten minutes before serving.
2. Bake until golden.
3. Whisk until smooth.
4. Chop everything finely.
